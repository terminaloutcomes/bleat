Bleat is an app for listening to audiobooks from an Audiobookshelf server chosen and controlled by you or your server administrator.

## Your library controls the content

Bleat does not provide, select, or host audiobooks.

Titles, descriptions, cover art, and audio shown in Bleat come from your Audiobookshelf server.

The user, server owner and administrator decides what is available and they are responsible for determining whether that content is suitable for each listener.
